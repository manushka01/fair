Warm Homes Local Grant (WHLG) The Complete Guide for Homeowners, Private Renters & Social Housing Tenants

The Warm Homes: Local Grant scheme is one of the UK Government's flagship initiatives designed to help households reduce energy bills, improve home comfort, and cut carbon emissions – all at no cost to eligible applicants.

If you live in England and struggle with high energy bills, poor insulation, or inefficient heating, this scheme could provide fully funded home improvements worth thousands of pounds.

In this comprehensive guide, we explain everything you need to know – from eligibility and funding to what measures are available and how to apply.

What is the Warm Homes Local Grant?

The Warm Homes Local Grant is a government-funded scheme delivered through local authorities to improve the energy efficiency of homes across England.

It focuses on supporting low-income households living in energy-inefficient properties, helping them upgrade their homes with modern insulation and low-carbon heating systems.

This programme is:

Fully funded

no contribution required for eligible households

Locally delivered

through councils and approved installers such as ECO4 Government Grant

Whole-house focused

tailored improvements, not just single measures

The scheme launched in 2025 and is part of a wider government strategy to improve housing standards and tackle fuel poverty.
