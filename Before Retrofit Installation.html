Prepare your home before retrofit installation with clear guidance on what to expect during the process. Whether you're having a heat pump installed, loft or wall insulation fitted, or solar panels added to your property, taking the right steps beforehand helps ensure a smoother installation. This home retrofit preparation guide explains how to get your property ready, what access may be needed, and how to prepare for temporary disruption to heating, hot water, or electricity during the work.

What To Expect During Your Retrofit Journey

You make your initial enquiry to improve the energy efficiency of your home.

We validate your property's eligibility and carry out an initial outreach call to understand your needs.

A qualified assessor surveys your home, designs a suitable retrofit package, and identifies improvement measures.

Your home is prepared and our installers carry out the agreed works to a high standard.

Every installation is quality checked, and you receive a full handover pack explaining your new systems.

Our team remains on hand after installation to answer any questions and provide ongoing support.
