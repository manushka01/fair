Free Insulation Grant Scheme

Free insulation grants for your home are available through a UK government scheme, aimed at improving energy efficiency and reducing carbon emissions. These grants are often provided to homeowners, private renters, and social housing tenants to help cover the cost of installing insulation measures such as loft insulation, cavity wall insulation, and solid wall insulation.

The availability of grants and the eligibility criteria can vary depending on factors such as location, household income, and the type of property. Typically, these grants are part of broader energy efficiency programs like the Energy Company Obligation (ECO).

By offering financial assistance for insulation upgrades, these grants aim to make homes more energy-efficient, reduce energy bills for occupants, and contribute to the UK's efforts to combat climate change. To find out whether you are eligible, simply complete our online form and one of our representatives will be in touch shortly.

Types of Insulation Available

Cavity Wall Insulation −

Cavity wall insulation is installed in the gap (cavity) between the inner and outer walls of a building. It helps to reduce heat loss through the walls by filling this gap with insulating material. Typically, cavity wall insulation is made from materials such as mineral wool, foam boards, or polystyrene beads. It can significantly improve the energy efficiency of a building and reduce heating costs.
Read more about cavity wall insulation

External Wall Insulation +

External wall insulation adds an insulating layer, plus a weatherproof finish, to the outside of a building, improving thermal performance without reducing internal room sizes.

Floor Insulation +

Floor insulation reduces heat loss through the ground floor of a property, helping to keep rooms warmer and cutting energy bills.

Internal Wall Insulation +

Internal wall insulation is fitted to the inside face of external walls, ideal for properties where external insulation isn't suitable, such as listed buildings.

Loft Insulation +

Loft insulation is laid between and over joists in the loft space to stop heat escaping through the roof, one of the quickest ways to cut heat loss.

Room in Roof Insulation +

Room in roof insulation is fitted to the sloped ceilings, walls, and floor of converted loft rooms to keep them warm in winter and cool in summer.
