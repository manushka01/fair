Solar Panels −

Solar panels, also known as photovoltaic (PV) panels, are made up of many small units called solar cells. These cells are usually made of silicon, a semiconductor material.

Photovoltaic Effect +

When sunlight hits the solar cells, it knocks electrons loose from their atoms, creating an electric current. This is known as the photovoltaic effect.

Direct Current (DC) Electricity +

The electricity generated by the solar cells flows as direct current (DC), which is then converted into usable alternating current (AC) by an inverter.

Usage or Storage +

The AC electricity can be used immediately to power your home, fed back into the grid, or stored in a battery for later use.

Monitoring and Control +

A monitoring system tracks how much energy your panels are generating and using, helping you keep an eye on your savings and system performance.

Overall, home solar panels offer a renewable and sustainable way to generate electricity, helping to power your home and reducing your electricity bills over time.
